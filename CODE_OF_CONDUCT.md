# Code of Conduct

Keep project communication technical, respectful, and constructive.

- Discuss code and behavior rather than people.
- Assume good faith while still being precise about defects.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, or discriminate against contributors.
- Keep bug reports reproducible and reviews focused on the proposed change.
- Respect licensing and provenance of historical third-party material.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that materially violate these
rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
